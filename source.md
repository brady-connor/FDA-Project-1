# Data source

Source: cbratkovics/nba-game-logs on Hugging Face.

This project uses five regular-season Parquet files: 2021-22 through 2025-26. Together they contain 130,414 player-game rows across 30 NBA teams and 26 source columns. Coverage runs from 2021-10-19 through 2026-04-12.

One row represents one player in one regular-season game. DNP rows are excluded by the source. Playoffs, play-in, preseason, All-Star games, and the NBA Cup final are excluded.
