About the data and how every number is computed

  • Source. The Hugging Face data set cbratkovics/nba-game-logs (player box scores, 2021-22 to 2025-26, regular season only), itself built from Eoin Moore's Kaggle NBA Dataset: Box Scores and Stats (CC0), which is derived from NBA.com. scripts/build_data.py converts it to data/player_games.csv.
  • One row is one player in one game. The time column is season and the group column is player (also team).
  • Rows dropped. Rows with zero or missing minutes or points are removed, because those players did not record a box score, and so are rows in games where a win or loss cannot be worked out.
  • Per-game averages = total of a stat divided by the number of player-games in the group. Team scoring per game = points summed over all players of a team in a game, averaged over team-games.
  • True shooting % = points / (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)). Group values use summed points, FGA and FTA, not an average of single-game percentages.
  • Win or loss is not in the source file, so the script derives it: the team with more total points in a game (summed over its players) won. Win rate = wins / team-games (or player-games on the dashboard).
  • Triple-double = a player-game with at least 10 points, 10 rebounds and 10 assists. Monthly scoring averages team-game points by calendar month. Leader tables require at least 40 games played in the season (and 15 points per game for true shooting).
  • Every figure on this page is calculated in your browser from the CSV when the page loads, so it always matches the data and the dashboard.
